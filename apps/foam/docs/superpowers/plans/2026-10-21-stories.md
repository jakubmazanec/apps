# Stories (Foam Phase 6, Spec 1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development
> (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A story is a titled piece of content that a script gives in an effect; it is achieved once
across all runs, a toast shows it, the log keeps its line, a Stories window in the night's menu
lists the night's finds and opens over the log at the end, and the collection of titles is saved in
the browser's storage.

**Architecture:** `core/stories.ts` owns the persisted collection, as `core/settings.ts` owns the
volumes; the night holds a reference to it and its own list of finds, and `findStory`/`hasStory` on
`core/night.ts` are pure on the night. The night screen compares the night's list with what it has
handled each frame, as it compares the status string, queues a `Toast` per find and saves. The
checker runs every effect on every check night, gathers the titles and runs everything twice, on an
empty and on a full collection. `StoriesWindow` is a modal built per opening, from the menu and from
the log screen's `onShow`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 6 (`exactOptionalPropertyTypes`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), Tellurion
(`PersistedStore`, `Fader`, `Scheduler.after`, `Modal`, `Container`, `Panel`, `Text`, `Button`,
`wrapText`), zod 4, Pixi.js 8, Vitest 4 (`unit` in Node, `browser` in Playwright's Chromium),
Prettier 3, Turborepo.

**Spec:** `apps/foam/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-stories-design.md`

Runs after the plan `apps/foam/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-end-of-the-night-and-log.md`, which
is built: `core/log.ts` holds `LogEntry`, `logText`, `logChoice` and `formatLog`; `core/hours.ts`
holds `isNightOver` and `formatTime`; `screens/logScreen.ts`, `screens/textBlock.ts` and the shared
menu in `screens/menuModal.ts` exist; `tests/nightScreenHelpers.tsx` has `describeEntry`,
`getMenuButton`, `openSpot`, `endStory`, `closeStory` and `restartAt`.

  lines. Never use `git stash`. Commands run from `apps/foam` unless a step says otherwise.

  the task, plus `npm run typecheck` and `npx eslint source tests scripts`. The whole suite (about
  nine minutes) is CI's, after the last task. Lint expectation: 0 errors; the known warnings of

  alphabetically within their group (`perfectionist/sort-classes`), and so are the keys of a

## Review Focus

- **Quit to menu with a toast showing.** The toast must go with the screen and the find must be in
  storage, saved by the frame's handling or by `onHide`. Pinned in Task 4.
- **A full collection with an empty night.** The Stories window counts the night's finds only: after
  a second New Game it reads `Stories  0 found` and `None yet` while the collection holds two
  titles. Pinned in Task 4.
- **The toast on the narrowest screen.** A title of 31 characters wraps to two lines at the text
  width of 114, and the toast is 138 wide and 59 tall, inside the screen. Pinned in Task 3.
- **An achieved story that hides the only free choice.** The checker reports no way out on the full
  collection, so a player can never be stuck in a window. Pinned in Task 2.
- **The Stories window over an empty log.** A night that found nothing, or no night at all, shows
  `Stories  0 found` and `None` above the log, and Close shows the log with nothing focused. Pinned
  in Task 4.
