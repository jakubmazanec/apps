# The Phone (Foam Phase 8, Spec 1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development
> (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The player has a phone: the author's messages arrive when the night is looked at between
windows, each announced by a toast and a chime, a Phone button in a new bottom row counts the unread
ones and opens the oldest in the story window, a reply is a choice, and the night saves what has
arrived and what was read.

**Architecture:** A message is content with a key, a sender, a condition on the night and a script
written with `defineScript`; the night keeps two lists of keys, unread and read, which the save
carries. The night screen receives messages in `actOnNight`, right after its check that the night is
over, and opens one through `openStory` under the path `phone › key`, so the log, the texts read and
the save work unchanged; the window's end marks it read. The scene area loses a bottom row on every
screen, and the Phone button stands at its right end as Menu stands at the top row's. The checker
walks every message's script as it walks a place's, and runs the condition on every check night.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 6 (`exactOptionalPropertyTypes`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), Tellurion
(`Button`, `Text`, `Dialogue`, `GameAssets`, `AudioMixer`), zod 4, Vitest 4 (`unit` in Node,
`browser` in Playwright's Chromium), Prettier 3, Turborepo.

**Spec:** `apps/foam/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-phone-design.md`

Runs after phase 7 is built (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-saving-and-continue.md`): `pick`,
`events`, `meet` and `hasMet` on the night, the saved night and `restoreNight`, `startFrom` with a
seed and `findSeed` in the test helpers. The direction document already carries the Phone row, the
phase 7 review and the phase 8 paragraph (commit `03966f53`); the last task only states that the
spec is built.

Five things the spec implies but its file table or its examples do not say, settled here:

- **Windows lay out between the two rows** (decision 5), so the three layouts that centre a panel
  under the top row, `storyWindow.ts`, `logScreen.ts` and `listWindow.ts`, gain a bottom padding of
  the row; without it a full-height window on the narrow screen lies on the Phone button. The
  Collection window on the main menu gets shorter by the row too: one shape of the area for every
  screen, as the spec's rejected list says.
- **`scripts/list-stand-ins.mjs` lists the phone folder,** or "Done when" cannot hold.
- **The test world's two lowest things move up** in `nightScreen.browser.test.ts`: the bottom row
  covers the picture's lowest 24 art pixels, and no scene button can be centred there.
- **A jump-in at 20:30 without the bottle brings three messages, not two:** the hello comes on every
  night's first look, with the shop and the bet. The hint and the wrong number come by the seed, so
  the browser tests play seeds whose nights keep them silent, or seed 7, whose night sends the hint
  and no wrong number.
- **"One row shorter"** is the bottom row's 24 art pixels: two lines of text on every page.

Decisions the spec leaves to the plan:

- `receiveMessages` walks `messages` in order; a message whose key is in either list is skipped
  without its condition read; otherwise `arrives(night)` is read, and a true pushes the key onto
  `unread` and the message onto the result.
- `getUnreadMessage` is `messages.find(({key}) => key === night.phone.unread[0])`.
- `readMessage` finds the key's index in `unread`, and with one takes it out and pushes it onto
  `read`.
- `dropUnknownMessages` builds a `Set` of the content's keys and, for each list, warns for each key
  not in the set with the spec's line, then puts the kept keys back in place with
  `splice(0, Infinity, ...kept)` as `reloadCollection` does, so a night that shares the lists sees
  the change. The warning carries the `no-console` directive `save.ts` uses.
