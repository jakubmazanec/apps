# The Picture (Foam Phase 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development
> (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use
> checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The night screen and the main menu show a moving picture of the bar, drawn by a shader of
Foam's own into a texture of art size, 30 steps a second, at half speed while a window is open.

**Architecture:** `PlacePicture` is a `Renderable` that checks the shader through WebGL itself,
owns a `RenderTexture` of the screen's art size and an off-screen mesh with the picture's shader,
and draws the mesh into the texture once per picture step. `core/pictureShader.ts` holds the GLSL
every picture shares (pixel, hash, noise, tone, glow, inks) and joins it with a place's own GLSL;
`content/barPicture.ts` is the bar. The clock is two pure functions.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, GLSL ES 3.00 (WebGL 2), Pixi.js 8.19 (`Mesh`, `MeshGeometry`, `Shader`,
`GlProgram`, `UniformGroup`, `RenderTexture`, `renderer.extract.pixels`), Tellurion, Vitest 4.

**Spec:** `apps/foam/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-picture-design.md`

Runs after the look plan (`2026-10-05-look.md`): it relies on `palette`, the title in
`mainMenuScreen`'s contents and the theme.

## Global Constraints

- Only files under `apps/foam/` change. Nothing under `apps/somewhere/` or `packages/tellurion/`;
  no shader support goes into Tellurion; the CRT filter is not touched.
- New inks, appended to `palette` after `rose`, in this order: `pink 0xff6281`, `plum 0x5e0960`,
  `magenta 0xc20265`, `blue 0x004cec`, `cyan 0x00c9ff`, `mint 0x00ffb3`. The shader's `uPalette`
  follows the order of `Object.values(palette)`.
- Every pixel of a picture is exactly one palette colour: no blending, no alpha, no
  interpolation. The checkerboard is fixed to the screen: a pixel's ink in a two-ink tone follows
  from `(x + y) % 2`, and movement only changes the intensity fed to `tone`.
- `PICTURE_STEPS_PER_SECOND = 30`. Speed is `1`, or `0.5` while `screen.ui.topOverlay !== null`.
- The bar is written against a design of 480 × 270; a design point `(x, y)` lies at
  `(x × width / 480, y × height / 270)`. Positions and lengths stretch; halo and core radii, line
  thickness, noise cells, dust size and sway, speeds in pixels a second and the checkerboard stay
  in screen pixels.
- All shape, ladder and movement numbers are the spec's tables, copied, not re-derived.
- No per-pixel loop longer than a few dozen steps; no per-pixel computation in TypeScript.
- `update` never throws; everything that can fail fails in the constructor.
- Same code style, lint expectations and test rules as the look plan's Global Constraints (`let`
  for locals, `.js` imports, `vitest` object, no `expect` in `beforeAll`, game modules imported
  after `bootGame`, 0 eslint errors). Commit at the end of each task, one short imperative
  sentence, no prefix, no trailer.
- Commands run from `apps/foam` unless a step says otherwise.

## Review Focus

- **Image flipped or offset by one.** A render texture's y axis and pixel centres are easy to get
  wrong. The proof test checks the band's row on two steps and the checkerboard parity at the
  corners `(0, 0)` and `(width − 1, height − 1)` (Task 1).
- **Floating drift of the clock over a long night.** Time is accumulated in seconds; the shader gets
  the whole-number step. Pinned by `getPictureStep` tests and the "update draws once per step"
  test at speed 0.5 (Task 1).
- **A screen size that is not a whole number of art pixels, or 0.** The texture is rounded up, and
  `resize(0, h)` keeps the last size. Pinned in Task 1.
- **A wide button on a narrow screen leaving its thing.** Pinned by the overlap tests at 195 × 350
  and 146 × 262 (Task 2).
- **A shader mistake that only shows on a real device.** Not testable here; the GLSL declares
  `highp` for `float` and `int` in both stages so the hash is the same everywhere, and the author
  checks a phone (Task 1 checkpoint, Task 4 hand-over).

**Interfaces:**

- Produces:
  - `getPictureStep.ts`: `PICTURE_STEPS_PER_SECOND = 30`;
    `advancePictureTime(time: number, deltaMs: number, speed: number): number` (seconds);
    `getPictureStep(time: number): number` (`Math.floor(time * 30)`).
  - `pictureShader.ts`: `createPictureShaderSource(picture: string): {vertex: string; fragment:
    string}`. Both start with `#version 300 es` and declare `precision highp float;` and
    `precision highp int;`.
    - Vertex: Pixi's mesh inputs (`in vec2 aPosition; in vec2 aUV;` and `uniform mat3
      uProjectionMatrix, uWorldTransformMatrix, uTransformMatrix;`), position
      `uProjectionMatrix * uWorldTransformMatrix * uTransformMatrix * vec3(aPosition, 1.0)`, and
      `out vec2 vPosition = aPosition` (art pixels, y down). The pixel comes from `vPosition`, not
      from `gl_FragCoord`, so a render target's flip cannot move it.
    - Fragment uniforms: `uniform vec2 uSize; uniform int uStep; uniform vec3
      uPalette[PALETTE_SIZE];` and `out vec4 finalColor;`. Generated from `palette.ts`:
      `PALETTE_SIZE` and one `const int INK_<NAME>` per key, in key order (`INK_BLACK = 0` …
      `INK_MINT = 12`).
    - Provided GLSL: `struct Ladder {int lo; int hi; int top;}` with `const Ladder WARM`,
      `DEEP`, `LIGHT` as the spec's ladder table; `vec3 ink(int index)`;
      `float hash(ivec2 cell, int seed)` in `[0, 1)`, from unsigned whole-number arithmetic only;
      `float noise(vec2 position, vec2 cellSize, int seed)`, smooth value noise on `hash`;
      `vec3 tone(vec3 below, float intensity, Ladder ladder, float jitter)` exactly as the spec
      defines it, where the two-ink tones give the lower ink on even `x + y`;
      `float glow(float distance, float radius)` = `1 / (1 + d² / r²)`; `ivec2 toScreen(vec2
      design)` = `ivec2(floor(design * uSize / vec2(480.0, 270.0)))`.
    - `main` computes `ivec2 pixel = ivec2(floor(vPosition))`, `vec2 point = (vec2(pixel) + 0.5) *
      vec2(480.0, 270.0) / uSize` and `float t = float(uStep) / 30.0`, and writes
      `finalColor = vec4(picture(pixel, point, t), 1.0)`.
    - A place's GLSL defines `vec3 picture(ivec2 pixel, vec2 point, float t)`.
  - `PlacePicture` (in `screens/placePicture.ts`), the spec's class: `constructor({picture})`,
    `readonly view`, `speed = 1`, `destroy()`, `resize(width, height)`, `update(ticker)`.
  - `Place.picture: string`; `samplePlace.picture = barPicture`.
  - `NightScreenContents.picture: PlacePicture` replaces `background`.

3. A `pixi.MeshGeometry` with positions `[0, 0, w, 0, w, h, 0, h]`, matching UVs and indices

4. A `pixi.RenderTexture.create({width: 1, height: 1, resolution: 1, scaleMode: 'nearest',

5. `update(ticker)`: `#time = advancePictureTime(#time, ticker.deltaMS, speed)`; when
   `getPictureStep(#time)` differs from the last drawn step or the picture is marked dirty, set
   `uStep` and `uSize`, call `group.update()`, and
   `game.app.renderer.render({container: mesh, target: texture, clear: true})`.
6. `resize(width, height)`: ignore a width or height of 0 or less; otherwise `Math.ceil` both,
   resize the texture and set the geometry's positions (and the uniform size), mark dirty.
7. `destroy()`: the view with its children, the mesh, the geometry, the shader, and the texture

The class comment says why the check is its own (Pixi only logs a failed shader), why the texture

- [ ] **Step 3: Write the bar**

`barPicture.ts` defines `picture(pixel, point, t)` with the spec's shapes, in the spec's order
(shelf, counter, haze, lamp cones, halos, dust, light on the floor, door, tables, glints), each
shape a GLSL function of its own that takes and returns the colour so far, and the six movements
from the spec's table. The shapes' numbers, ladders and jitters are the spec's tables; this plan
decides only what the spec leaves to it:

- **Lamps.** A small GLSL array of three lamps (design centre, radius, reach, phase `p`). `power`
  and the back lamp's flicker (`hash(ivec2(floor(t * 7.0), 0), seed) < 0.07`) are computed once
  per pixel per lamp. The white cross is drawn at `toScreen(centre)` in screen pixels, on pixels
  that share its x or its y within twice the radius; a flickering lamp has no cross.
- **Dust.** Each cone is cut into vertical lanes 6 screen pixels wide. A lane's sink time (30 to
  80 s) and phase come from `hash(ivec2(lane, lamp), seed)`. In the lane's moving coordinate
  `y' = y − reach × t / sinkTime` (wrapped over the reach), the lane is cut into cells; a cell's
  hash says whether it has a dot and its height. The dot sits at the lane's middle plus
  `round(2 × sin(...))`, so it stays inside its own lane: the pixel checks one cell only. Choose the
  cell height and chance so that a cone has about 0.9 dots per pixel of its reach; a quarter of
  the dots are `white`, the rest `mint`.
- **Glints.** A loop over the 22 dashes (within the "few dozen steps" limit). Dash `i` takes its x
  on the counter, height above the edge (3–8), length (3–11), period (1.1–3.5 s) and colour from
  hashes of `i`; it is on in period `n = floor(t / period)` when `hash(ivec2(i, n), seed) < 0.55`.
- **Bottles, street, tram, haze.** As the spec's movement table, with each 5-pixel column's phase
  and rate from `hash(ivec2(column, 0), seed)`.

Every colour reaches the result through `tone` or a single `ink`, never mixed.
